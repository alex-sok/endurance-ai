Homepage Brain OS product concepts — 15 October 2026

These are responsive HTML/CSS/SVG mockups, not captured screenshots from Nova
or a live customer environment. All chart values, customer labels, records,
conversations, drafts, and operational totals inside the mockup are synthetic.
The mockup has no backend, sends no messages, and makes no operational changes.

Workflow evidence already documented in this repository:
- app/brain-os/use-cases/content.ts: BEFORE_AFTER (load lookup, order drafts,
  carrier history, morning unbilled-load digest), ROLE_TABS (billing, leadership,
  questions in chat, quiet customers), REVENUE (lane history).
- app/brain-os/technical/content.ts: JOBS (scheduled operational and leadership
  digests), TOOLS / RUNS (systems of record, Teams and Slack, source-backed reads).
These describe the reference logistics deployment in September 2026. This
change does not assert a new audit of its current production state.

The separate “Part of the working day” block uses existing published figures:
- 35 people who asked in the seven days to 24 September 2026: ADOPTION.people.
- 94 orders created 21–25 September 2026, closing at 11:09 Eastern on Friday;
  9 later voided: ACTIONS.chart.
- 153 operating rules kept from 24 July, counted 21 September 2026: HEADLINE.
These are labeled as one deployment, dated, and linked to /brain-os/use-cases.

Mockup data consistency:
- Billing example: latest sample week 126 delivered, 108 billed, 18 unbilled.
  12 need proof of delivery and 6 need invoice review; 12/18 = two-thirds.
- Delivery example: latest sample week 1,000 deliveries, 960 on time, 40 late.
  26 appointment delays + 10 transit delays + 4 other = 40; 26/40 = 65%.
  The 8-week on-time series ends at 96% and starts at 92% (+4 percentage points).
- Customer example: 94, 108, and 126 days since last shipment. Prior 90-day
  volume 24 + 17 + 12 = 53 loads; no shipments in the latest 90 days.

Visual direction: warm white, charcoal, muted olive; precise typography and
vector graphs. No fades, gradients, or autoplay. Tabs, period switches, source
tables, and example draft previews are interactive and keyboard accessible.
